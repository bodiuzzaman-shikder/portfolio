01 About Me

I am an innovative and versatile electrical and mechanical design engineer with hands-on experience in robotics, automation, power systems and advanced manufacturing. As a Design Engineer (R&D) at Adex Engineering Ltd., I design HT (11–33 kV) and LT switchgear, instrument transformers and power transformers (0.15–28 MVA) for the power sector of Bangladesh, combining SOLIDWORKS modelling with MATLAB-based engineering calculation and FEA/CFD validation.

Driven by a passion for research and education, I pair strong academic training with leadership from international competitions such as the University Rover Challenge. I hold five professional SOLIDWORKS certifications, including SOLIDWORKS Champion. I am now seeking a funded graduate research opportunity where I can contribute impactful solutions at the intersection of mechatronics, robotics and sustainable energy systems.
