01. About Me

I am a Design Engineer (R&D) at Adex Engineering Ltd., where I design HT (11–33 kV) and LT switchgear, instrument transformers and power transformers (0.15–28 MVA) for the power sector of Bangladesh. My work sits at the intersection of electrical power engineering and mechanical design — from 33 kV metal-clad AIS panels to in-house MATLAB engineering software.

Beyond industry, I led the mechanical teams of UIU Mars Rover and UIU CanSat, representing Bangladesh at the University Rover Challenge in Utah, USA — placing 9th (2023) and 5th (2024) worldwide — and at CanSat 2025, USA. I completed my BSc in Electrical & Electronic Engineering at United International University with an 81% First-Class result, and my capstone underwater vehicle Octopus won Best Capstone Project.

I am now seeking graduate research opportunities and advanced design engineering roles where I can contribute rigorous, hands-on R&D experience.
